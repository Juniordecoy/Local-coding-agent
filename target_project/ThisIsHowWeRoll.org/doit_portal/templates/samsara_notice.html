Samsara Notice

Dear Valued Driver Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to your safety and the protection of our fleet, we are implementing Samsara AI dashcams in all do it outdoors media vehicles. These advanced systems are designed to help protect you on the road and promote safer driving practices.

Purpose of Implementation

The Samsara AI dashcam system serves multiple purposes:

  • Protecting drivers from false claims in case of accidents
  • Promoting safer driving habits
  • Reducing accident rates and insurance claims
  • Providing valuable coaching opportunities for professional development

Driver Behaviors Monitored by the Samsara System

The Samsara AI dashcams use advanced technology to detect and record specific driving behaviors.

1. Distracted Driving

  • Using a handheld electronic device while driving
  • Eating or drinking while the vehicle is in motion
  • Looking away from the road for extended periods

2. Following Distance Violations

  • Tailgating or maintaining unsafe following distances from vehicles ahead

3. Seatbelt Compliance

  • Driving without properly fastened seatbelts

4. Signs of Fatigue or Drowsiness

  • Head nodding
  • Prolonged yawning
  • Face rubbing

5. Defensive Driving Actions

  • The system also records positive defensive driving behaviors

What Happens After Detection

  1. You may receive real-time in-cab audio alerts
  2. Safety events may be reviewed by management
  3. Footage may be used for coaching purposes

Privacy and Support

These cameras are not designed for continuous monitoring but rather to capture safety-related events. The system is here to protect you and improve our safety culture.

If you have questions please contact your supervisor or the Field Safety Manager.

*1. The purpose of the Samsara AI dashcam is to promote safe driving habits.

*2. Samsara's distracted driving detection can identify cell phone use.

*3. The Following Distance Violation feature detects tailgating.

*4. Drivers may receive real-time in-cab audio alerts.

*5. Have you read and understood all of the information provided?
