Photo Tips and Tricks

  • Make sure your size is set at the lowest possible setting in your camera
  • The entire truck and vinyl must be visible and legible
  • Make sure the vinyls aren’t obstructed by trees, poles, etc.
  • Put traffic between yourself and the truck, in other words dont take photos of traffic behind the truck.
  • If parked along a major street, take the time to safely walk across the street to take photos of traffic driving by (don’t put traffic behind the truck)
  • Is the truck close enough in the frame to be legible, but not so close to miss the traffic/surroundings?
  • NO CHILDREN!
